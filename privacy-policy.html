Privacy Policy

Independent WhatsApp Messaging App
Effective date: 11 November 2026
Project maintainer: AHCoderCode
Software support: GitHub issue tracker

Encryption notice: This service is not currently end-to-end encrypted. Message contents and some metadata are stored in readable form by the connected Supabase service. Do not treat this as a secure or confidential messenger.

1. Who operates this app

This is an independently operated messaging application whose source is published at github.com/AHCoderCode/whatsapp. It is not the official WhatsApp service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.

2. Information processed

  • Account information: username, internal app ID, display name, profile/About text, profile image and authentication identifiers. Password authentication is handled by Supabase Auth; the app does not display or retrieve your password.
  • Messages and content: message text, message type, timestamps, delivery/read-receipt records, reactions, replies, polls, events, status updates, channel/community content and files or voice notes that you choose to send.
  • Contacts and safety: contact requests, accepted-contact relationships, group memberships, block relationships and reports you submit.
  • Calls: call participants, call time, status, media type and duration metadata. During calls, the app requests microphone/camera access and uses WebRTC network transport. WebRTC transport encryption is not the same as verified end-to-end encryption against the service infrastructure.
  • Device and service data: push notification tokens/subscriptions, linked-device/session records, IP/network information processed by hosting and TURN relay providers, and local preferences stored by your browser or app.

3. How information is used

Information is used to create and authenticate accounts, deliver messages and attachments, sync contacts and groups, show read receipts, operate calls, deliver notifications, prevent abuse, enforce blocks, process reports, troubleshoot faults and maintain service security.

4. Service providers

The app uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage and realtime services; Vercel for hosting; Firebase Cloud Messaging for supported Android push notifications; and a TURN relay provider for some voice/video connections. These providers process information needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. The app does not sell personal information or use message content for advertising.

5. Storage and security

Access is restricted through authenticated sessions, database row-level security, private storage policies and encrypted network transport. These safeguards do not make this service E2EE: the service backend can access plaintext message records and uploaded files. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing and visibility

Your profile information, messages, attachments, group content, status updates and channel posts are shared with the recipients, group members or audiences you select. Push notifications are designed not to include message bodies. Reports are stored for safety review. A recipient may copy, save or capture content outside the app.

7. Retention

Content is retained while the relevant account or conversation records remain in the connected database/storage, subject to disappearing-message settings, expiry cleanup, user deletion actions and provider backups/retention. Report and security records may be retained as needed to investigate abuse and protect the service.

8. Your choices and rights

You can manage profile details, notification preferences, read receipts, blocked contacts and disappearing-message settings in the app. For software issues, use the public issue tracker, but do not post passwords, private conversations or sensitive details there.

Account deletion is not currently available. Account identity and some data tables are shared with another project, so deleting the shared Supabase identity could affect that other service. A separate tested deletion process has not yet been implemented. Do not publish this app on Google Play until a working in-app and web-based deletion path is available. See Account deletion information.

9. Children

The service is not designed for unsupervised use by young children. Users must meet applicable minimum-age and consent requirements in their country. The app does not intentionally request an upload of the device address book.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the service's data practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised. For software issues, use the project issue tracker; do not include confidential information in public reports.
